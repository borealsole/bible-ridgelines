How to write the list

Separate entries with commas or new lines. Each entry can be:

  • H430, G26: a Strong’s number
  • ἀγάπη, אלהים: the original word (accents and vowel points optional)
  • agape: a transliteration
  • love, steadfast love: an English gloss
  • lov*, *love: * stands for any letters
  • fam:G25: the whole Strong’s family of a word; root:G26: words sharing its root chain
  • "λόγος θεός", "בראשית ברא", "H3068 H6635": a phrase in quotes, i.e. those words in that order. Hebrew prefixes and the Greek article may come between them, and * stands for any one word. Phrases match dictionary forms, and a gloss of several words is joined with _ (steadfast_love)
  • G25 + G26: with “one ridge per entry”, + joins terms into one ridge
  • gloss:, lemma:, translit: and strongs: say how to read a term
